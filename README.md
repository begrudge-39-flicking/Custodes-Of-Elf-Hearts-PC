## 🕹️ About

Custodes of Elf Hearts is an adult fantasy tower-defense RPG developed by Backflip WildCAT and published by LewdLoco. Set beneath the World Tree, it follows a half-human, half-elf guardian protecting an underground elven settlement from black sludge creatures and a traitor within his organization. Players place and upgrade elf-girl turrets, manage resources, use character and goddess skills, and fight varied enemies threatening the World Tree. The story combines comedic daily-life scenes, visual-novel storytelling, relationship development, and adult events with strategic battles.

A strong fit for adult players who enjoy anime-style fantasy, tower-defense strategy, visual novels, and relationship-driven RPGs.

## ✨ Features

- 🌳 **World Tree Setting** — Explore an underground elven society built beneath the roots of the World Tree.
- 📖 **Story-Driven Scenes** — Follow lighthearted daily-life episodes, dramatic revelations, and visual-novel events.
- ⚔️ **Tactical Skills** — Spend resources wisely and unleash powerful character and goddess abilities.
- 🧝 **Six Elf Heroines** — Build relationships with six distinct elf characters, each with unique personalities.
- 🛡️ **Tower Defense Combat** — Place character turrets, strengthen their abilities, and repel advancing enemies.
- 🦠 **Enemy Variety** — Adapt your defenses against black sludge creatures with different forms and abilities.
